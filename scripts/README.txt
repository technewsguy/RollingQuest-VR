ROLLING QUEST VR  {VERSION}
https://github.com/technewsguy/RollingQuest-VR

Plays Rolling Quest (https://krampus1721.itch.io/rollingquest) in a SteamVR headset.

INSTALL
 1. Unzip everything into the Rolling Quest folder (the one with RollingQuest.exe).
 2. Double-click "Rolling Quest VR - Add to Steam.bat".
    It adds "Rolling Quest VR" to your Steam library and SteamVR library (with artwork) and puts a
    shortcut on your desktop. Steam closes and restarts while it runs.
 3. With your headset connected, start "Rolling Quest VR" from Steam, SteamVR or the desktop.
    The FIRST start takes a minute or two while BepInEx prepares the game (it needs an internet
    connection that one time). After that it's quick.

CONTROLS
 Gamepad (PS4 / Xbox) and keyboard work as in the normal game. Index / Touch / Vive controllers act as a gamepad:
   Left thumbstick   roll / turn, menus          Right A   jump / accept       Right B   back
   Left A            action                      Left B    pause
   Right trigger     look up                     Left trigger  look down
   Left stick click  switch follow / diorama view (gamepad: Share/Back, keyboard: F9)
   Right stick click recenter the view (keyboard: F8)
 Diorama view (the level as a model on a table): push the stick where the ball should go.
   Right stick left/right turns the model a quarter turn, up/down makes it bigger or smaller.

SETTINGS
 BepInEx\config\rollingquestvr.mod.cfg (created after the first start). EnableVR = false plays the normal flat game.

UNINSTALL
 Double-click "Rolling Quest VR - Uninstall.bat", then remove "Rolling Quest VR" from Steam
 (right-click > Manage > Remove non-Steam game from your library).
